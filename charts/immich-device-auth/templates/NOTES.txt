immich-device-auth is deploying.

1. Allow this redirect URI on Immich's OAuth client in your identity provider:

     {{ include "immich-device-auth.publicUrl" . }}/device/oauth/callback

   OAuth must also be enabled in Immich (Administration > Settings > Authentication).
   Without both, users can't approve requests.

2. Make sure phones can reach {{ include "immich-device-auth.publicUrl" . }}
{{- if not .Values.ingress.enabled }}
   (ingress.enabled is false: expose the "{{ include "immich-device-auth.fullname" . }}" Service yourself).
{{- end }}

3. Try it from any machine:

     curl -X POST {{ include "immich-device-auth.publicUrl" . }}/device/code \
       -d client_name="Test app" -d scope="album.read" -d ttl=600

   Open the returned verification_uri_complete on your phone and approve.

Logs: kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deploy/{{ include "immich-device-auth.fullname" . }}
